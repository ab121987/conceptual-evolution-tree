Conceptual Evolution Tree

A conceptual map exploring how designed artefacts and ideas inherit, mutate and move across scales. What can be learned by temporarily classifying the work in this way? Does a project operate at one scale, or across several? How has the research evolved from intervening within individual scales toward designing relationships between them?
