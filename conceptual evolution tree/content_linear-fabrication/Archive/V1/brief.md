# Linear Fabrication Sankey Diagram Brief

## Purpose
Create an animated Sankey diagram for a research website and REF-oriented audience.

The diagram should frame the problem of linear manufacturing:
matter is treated as inert, form is imposed on it, discourse between material and environment is removed, and waste/harms are externalised.

## Core narrative
Linear fabrication typically follows a one-way sequence:
Extraction → Processing → Manufacture → Distribution → Consumption → Disposal

This sequence produces dead-end waste streams and broader ecological/social harms.

## What the diagram should communicate
- matter is extracted and processed as a passive resource
- manufacturing imposes fixed form
- materials are often artificial and difficult to reintegrate
- consumption is shaped by overproduction, damage, obsolescence and fashion
- disposal leads to landfill, incineration, toxicity, pollution and carbon release
- any loops should appear weak, partial, or marginal rather than dominant

## Tone
Analytical, diagrammatic, critical, restrained.

## Not the intended style
Do not mimic the collage aesthetic of the reference image showing extract-make-consume-dispose.

## Useful reference qualities
From the mycelium Sankey precedent:
- elegant flow structure
- animated movement through the diagram
- clear differentiation of streams
- ability to show branching and looping

## Important difference from the precedent
This diagram should foreground failed closure, dead ends and externalised harms, rather than successful circularity.

## Audience
Researchers, assessors, design audience, REF/public communication audience.
