# UK Estimate Layer — initial populated examples

The estimate layer fills selected gaps without pretending they are measured UK national balances.

Initial estimate families added:
- iron-ore-equivalent input to UK steelmaking, scaled from MIT global iron-ore/steel ratio;
- limestone-equivalent input to GB cement production, scaled from MIT global limestone/cement ratio;
- UK apparent steel use split into construction, transport equipment, machinery and other manufacturing using MIT global downstream steel shares;
- steelmaking energy and CO2 using worldsteel global intensity benchmarks applied to UK crude steel production;
- UK steel scrap generation/reuse/other pathway using UK Steel Strategy approximate figures;
- packaging not recycled/other treatment derived from Defra packaging arising minus recycled tonnage.

Actual source-supported bridging flows were also added for UK roundwood to primary processing, crude steel production and cement production.

All estimate calculations are recorded row-by-row in `methodology_note`, with URLs in `associated_sources`.
